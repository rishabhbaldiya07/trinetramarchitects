    Frequently Asked Questions

    Before You Reach Out

    Everything You Need To Know

    Can't find what you're looking for? Send us your question directly on WhatsApp or through our contact form.

    What services does Trinetram Group offer?

    We offer end-to-end Architecture Design, Construction, Interior Design, Renovation, Turnkey Projects and Planning & Consultation — all delivered by in-house teams under a single studio, so design and execution never drift apart.

    How do I start a project with Trinetram Group?

    Simply fill out the enquiry form on our Contact page, call us, or message us on WhatsApp with a brief about your project. Our team will schedule an initial consultation to understand your requirements, site and budget before proposing next steps.

    How long does a typical project take to complete?

    Timelines vary with scope — a residential interior may take 3–5 months, while a full turnkey villa or commercial build can take 10–18 months. During your consultation, we'll share a realistic, project-specific schedule before work begins.

    Do you provide cost estimates before starting work?

    Yes. Every project begins with a detailed, itemised cost estimate covering design, materials, labour and finishes, so there are no hidden costs later. We revisit and confirm costing again once the design is finalised.

    What does a "Turnkey Project" include?

    A turnkey project covers everything from concept design and regulatory approvals to construction, interiors and furnishing — handed over to you fully ready to move in, managed on a single unified timeline.

    Which cities and regions do you work in?

    We're based in Madhya Pradesh and take on residential, commercial and hospitality projects across India. Reach out with your project location and our team will confirm feasibility and site-visit logistics.

    Can I make changes to the design after construction begins?

    Minor changes can usually be accommodated, though they may affect cost and timeline depending on the construction stage. We recommend finalising the design and material selections upfront to keep your project on schedule and on budget.

    What are your payment terms?

    Payments are structured in stages tied to project milestones (design approval, material procurement, construction phases, and handover). Exact terms are outlined in your project agreement before work begins.
