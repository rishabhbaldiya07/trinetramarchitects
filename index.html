    About Trinetram Group

    Design-Led. Engineering-Driven. Built to Endure.

    Three Decades of Designing Spaces. Building Trust Since 1988.

    Designing Spaces. Building Trust. Since 1988. Established in 1988, Trinetram Group is the parent brand of Trinetram Architects & Interiors, an architectural and interior design firm dedicated to creating thoughtful, functional, and timeless spaces. For over three decades, we have delivered architectural planning, interior design, working drawings, 3D visualizations, and design consultancy with a commitment to creativity, technical precision, and lasting value.

    At Trinetram, every project begins with understanding people, purpose, and context. Through a user-centric and contextual design approach, we create functional, timeless, and aesthetically refined spaces that balance creativity with technical precision. 35+ Years of Design Excellence • Established in 1988 • Designed for Today. Built for Tomorrow.
