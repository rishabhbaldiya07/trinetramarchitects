    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 14 August 2026

    Trinetram Group ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through this website (trinetramgroup.com) and related channels, including our enquiry form and WhatsApp. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

    1Information We Collect

    When you use our enquiry form, call, email or message us on WhatsApp, we may collect:

    • Your name, phone number and email address
    • Details you share about your project, location and requirements
    • Basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site running smoothly

    2How We Use Your Information

    We use the information you provide to:

    • Respond to your enquiry and discuss your project requirements
    • Prepare quotes, proposals and project documentation
    • Communicate with you by phone, email or WhatsApp regarding an ongoing or prospective project
    • Improve our website and services

    We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

    3How We Share Information

    We may share information with trusted vendors, consultants or contractors directly involved in delivering your project, and only to the extent necessary. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

    4Cookies & Website Data

    Our website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some site features may not work as intended.

    5Data Security

    We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

    6Data Retention

    We retain your information for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry, deliver a project, or comply with legal and accounting obligations, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

    7Your Rights

    You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us at any time by contacting us using the details below.

    8Changes to This Policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last revised.
